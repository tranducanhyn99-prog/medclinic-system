# MedClinic

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Vietnamese patients book appointments and review their records. Doctors examine patients and manage their queue. Reception staff check patients in and collect payments. Administrators manage specialties, doctors, accounts, inventory and reports. These roles and tasks are evidenced by the existing application and README.

## Product Purpose

A Vietnamese clinic management course project with a connected journey from appointment booking through reception, examination, prescription and billing.

## Operating Context

Desktop workstations for clinic operations; responsive web pages for patients. Existing dev/demo seed accounts and H2 support local demonstrations. SQL Server is the documented deployment database. No real patient data is needed for design verification.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve Spring MVC, Thymeleaf, Bootstrap, locally bundled Geist and Bootstrap Icons.
- Preserve routes, role authorization, CSRF, form validation, draft retention and all JavaScript hooks.
- Booking offers available 30-minute slots up to 14 days ahead. QR reception issues a queue number; doctors complete records and prescriptions; staff handle invoices.
- Preserve light/dark preference, keyboard navigation, accessible labels, responsive tables and reduced motion.
- Use existing content, assets and real model values. Do not invent clinic claims, statistics or capabilities.

## Brand Commitments

MedClinic name and existing logo remain. The user explicitly requests a design that does not look AI-generated. Avoid interchangeable card grids, ornamental gradients and decorative icons; establish deliberate typography, composition and hierarchy around the actual clinic tasks.

On 9 October 2026 the user approved the implemented composition and selected medical blue with clean white as the revised palette. Use blue for actions and wayfinding, retaining semantic status colors and both themes.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, server-rendered templates, locally bundled doctor photographs, and historical screenshots under docs/evidence. Current local browser rendering takes precedence over older screenshots.

## Product Principles

- Make the next patient or staff action easy to locate.
- Put dates, times, clinicians, fees and statuses ahead of decoration.
- Keep clinical and payment information accurate through the existing backend.
- Reuse shared components so all four roles belong to the same product.

## Design Execution

The user approved the Appointment desk direction and delegated direct implementation on 8 October 2026. This redesign is code-led; generated direction previews are critique references, not pixel specifications. No standing workflow preference is assumed for later projects.
